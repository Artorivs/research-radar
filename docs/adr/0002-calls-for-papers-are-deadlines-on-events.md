# Calls for papers are Submission deadlines on an Event, not Items

A conference's call for papers is modelled as a Submission deadline on the
Event, alongside its other Deadlines, rather than as an Item of its own. This
is the reverse of WikiCFP and most CFP aggregators. It keeps one Event with one
Score, Vote and Status instead of a CFP Item and a conference Item that drift
apart. A Call exists as an Item only when someone other than the Event's
organiser issues it, or when no Event is involved (special issues, edited
volumes).
