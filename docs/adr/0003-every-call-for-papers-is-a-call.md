# Every call for papers is a Call

Supersedes [0002](0002-calls-for-papers-are-deadlines-on-events.md).

A conference's or workshop's own call for papers is now a Call Item, like any
other invitation to submit work, rather than a Submission deadline on a
Conference Item. Arthur looks for calls in one place, and finding a
conference's call filed under Conference meant checking two lists. A Call for
an Event keeps that Event's dates and Location and joins its Series, so Votes
on last year's call still lift this year's. Conference, Workshop and Seminar
Items now come only from announcements without an open call, such as a
programme or registration notice.

At the same time, commentary and news posts became Items (Blog post and News)
instead of being dropped, so that the feeds' own content can be read and
voted on alongside Papers.
