# Research Radar

A personal radar that gathers academic papers, events, calls and opportunities
worldwide and ranks them against one researcher's interests.

## What the radar collects

**Item**:
Anything the radar collects and scores, whatever its Kind.
_Avoid_: Entry, listing, record, post

**Kind**:
The fixed classification of an Item: Paper, Conference, Workshop, Seminar, Call, Course, Award or Job.
_Avoid_: Type, category, format

**Event**:
An Item that takes place at a time and Location: a Conference, Workshop or Seminar.
_Avoid_: Meeting, gathering, activity

**Conference**:
An Event with multiple panels, usually over several days, normally with an open call for papers.
_Avoid_: Congress, symposium (as Kinds)

**Workshop**:
A small, paper-based Event, often by invitation or selective submission.
_Avoid_: Roundtable, colloquium (as Kinds)

**Seminar**:
An Event consisting of a single talk or session, including lecture series, whether online or in person.
_Avoid_: Webinar, lecture, talk

**Call**:
An invitation to submit work issued by someone other than an Event's own organiser, or tied to no Event at all: a journal special issue, an edited volume, or a working group's call to form a panel at another body's Conference.
_Avoid_: CFP (as a Kind), call for papers, solicitation

**Course**:
Structured teaching one enrols in: summer and winter schools, PhD courses, methods training.
_Avoid_: Summer school, winter school, training, bootcamp (as Kinds)

**Award**:
Money or recognition one applies for without an employment contract: grants, fellowships, prizes, scholarships.
_Avoid_: Grant, fellowship, prize, scholarship, funding (as Kinds)

**Job**:
A post with an employment contract; an ambiguous postdoctoral "fellowship" counts as a Job.
_Avoid_: Position, vacancy, opening

**Paper**:
A single scholarly work, however many Versions of it exist.
_Avoid_: Article, publication, preprint (as Kinds)

**Version**:
One appearance of a Paper at a Source, such as an SSRN preprint, an arXiv copy or the published journal article.
_Avoid_: Copy, edition, manifestation

**Series**:
The recurring identity linking successive Events, whether annual editions (the ESIL Annual Conference) or sessions of a lecture series.
_Avoid_: Recurring event, edition, programme

**Deadline**:
A dated cut-off attached to an Item; an Event may carry several.
_Avoid_: Due date, cut-off, closing date

**Submission deadline**:
A Deadline for sending work or an application (abstract, full paper, application); the only kind that triggers an Alert.
_Avoid_: CFP deadline

**Location**:
Where an Event happens, including whether it is in person, hybrid or online.
_Avoid_: Venue, place, format

**Past**:
An Item whose Event has taken place or whose last Submission deadline has passed; derived from dates, never set by the researcher.
_Avoid_: Expired, closed, archived

## People and interests

**Profile**:
One researcher's declared interests, timezone and thresholds, against which every Item is scored.
_Avoid_: User, account, subscriber

**Topic**:
A named research area within a Profile (e.g. international law); every Item is tagged with one or more.
_Avoid_: Keyword, tag, interest, category

**Adjacent**:
The catch-all Topic for Items that score highly but fit none of the Profile's named Topics.
_Avoid_: Other, misc, uncategorised

## Judgement

**Score**:
The model's judgement of an Item's relevance to a Profile, an integer from −10 to +10.
_Avoid_: Rating, relevance, rank

**Vote**:
The researcher's 👍 or 👎 on an Item, used to tune future Scores.
_Avoid_: Feedback, like, rating

**Status**:
The researcher's progress with an Item, with values depending on Kind: Paper (saved, read); Event (saved, submitted, attending); Call, Course, Award, Job (saved, applied).
_Avoid_: State, stage, progress

**Dismissed**:
A mark on any Item meaning "not for me now": it hides the Item and stops its Alerts, without affecting future Scores.
_Avoid_: Hidden, archived, ignored, 👎

## Delivery

**Digest**:
The weekly email listing Items at or above the Profile's thresholds.
_Avoid_: Newsletter, report, summary

**Alert**:
A one-off email warning that a relevant Submission deadline is 14 days away.
_Avoid_: Reminder, notification

**Source**:
An existing publisher of material (a database, journal, blog, society, job board or mailing list) that the radar reads from.
_Avoid_: Feed, provider, site, crawler
